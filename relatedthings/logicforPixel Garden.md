That's actually a great candidate for a senior-friendly cognitive game.

I looked at your Pix Shooter concept, and instead of making it a "shooter," I'd reposition it as a brain training and rehabilitation game. Research suggests that older adults respond much better to non-violent, colorful, casual games with slower pacing, large UI elements, and positive feedback than to traditional shooters.

Why this is a strong idea

This could evolve from a simple game into a digital therapeutic and cognitive wellness platform aimed at seniors, retirement homes, and rehabilitation centers. By transforming Pix Shooter into a calm, accessible experience focused on memory, attention, and reaction time, you'd be creating something with both social impact and real-world applicability while reusing much of your existing game's core mechanics. Research on games for older adults consistently emphasizes simplicity, clear visuals, slower pacing, positive feedback, and meaningful progression—all of which align well with this direction.
